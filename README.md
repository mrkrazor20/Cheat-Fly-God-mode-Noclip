# Krazor V-Powers: Mod Menu

Welcome to the **Krazor V-Powers** repository. This is a custom Roblox mod menu designed for testing gameplay mechanics, physics, and character controls.

---

## 🚀 Features
* **Fly Mode:** Freely move through the air in the direction of your camera, ignoring gravity. Can be toggled using the `E` key or via the user interface.
* **Speed Control:** Easily adjust your flight speed using the `- Speed` and `+ Speed` buttons (ranging from 25 to 300).
* **God Mode:** Automatically locks your character's health at maximum to prevent elimination.
* **Noclip:** Disables character collision parts, allowing you to walk straight through walls and obstacles.

---

## 🕹️ Controls & Usage
1. **Menu Interface:** Once the script runs in-game, a **KRAZOR MOD MENU** window will appear on your screen. You can drag and reposition it freely using the title bar.
2. **Fly Toggle:** Click the **Fly** button in the menu or press **`E`** on your keyboard.
3. **Speed Adjustment:** Use the speed buttons at the bottom of the menu panel to set your preferred movement speed.
